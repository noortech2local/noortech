/ 05 — GOOD TO KNOW

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED.

Simple technology.
Clear answers.

Everything you need to understand Noor before the first release.

01Which phones work with Noor?+

Noor is designed for modern NFC-enabled iPhone and Android devices. A detailed compatibility list will be published before launch.

02Does the bracelet need charging?+

No. Noor uses a passive NFC chip and contains no battery, screen or microphone.

03What information is stored on the bracelet?+

The bracelet holds a secure link. Personal content lives in your editable Noor profile, so you remain in control of what is visible.

04When will the City Series be available?+

The first Saudi City Series is currently in development. Join the list for sample previews, launch timing and early access.
