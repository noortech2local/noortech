A city,
abstracted.

Rather than copying the city literally, we translated it into line, rhythm, space, and order. Familiar landmarks are reassembled into a continuous pattern with both cultural recognition and modern ease.

Architecture as identity

Kingdom Centre, Al Faisaliah Tower, the Riyadh skyline, and Islamic arches become a restrained, wearable visual language. Architecture is no longer just a backdrop; it becomes part of identity.

01

Heritage without nostalgia

Traditional Islamic geometry is broken down and rearranged to avoid ornament for ornament's sake, expressing Middle Eastern culture in a more contemporary and considered way.

02

Designed for
daily wear.

From material and touch to pattern scale, every detail is designed for long-term wear. It works equally well as a travel keepsake or as a subtle cultural marker in an everyday look.
