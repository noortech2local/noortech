QUESTIONS, ANSWERED.

The essentials to know before Noor’s first release.

01Which phones work with Noor?+

Noor is designed for modern NFC-enabled iPhone and Android devices. A detailed compatibility list will be confirmed before launch.

02Does the bracelet need charging?+

No. Noor uses passive NFC technology and contains no battery, screen or microphone.

The NFC component points to a secure link. You decide what that link opens and can update it as your needs change.
